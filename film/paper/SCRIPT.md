# Voice-over script: paper_film.mp4 (2:00)

Each block starts when its slide appears. **On screen** lists the numbers you will see, so you can keep your place. **Say** is what to speak; **bold** marks the word to land on the reveal. Each "Say" fits its slide at about 2.5 spoken words a second, with numbers counted as spoken ("sixteen point seven percent" is four words).

---

## 0:00 – Title (7 s)
**On screen:** Equal Care Across Finland · the paper: Khalife et al., *Acta Oncologica* 2025
**Say:** Equal Care Across Finland. We start from one paper: a Nordic comparison of lung-cancer care.

## 0:07 – Survival (7 s)
**On screen:** five-year survival, men, 2018–2022: Finland **16.7%**, Denmark 26.2%, Iceland 28.4% · found at stage I: Finland **17%**, Denmark and Norway over 25%
**Say:** **Lowest** survival in the Nordics: 16.7 percent for men. Only 17 percent found at stage one.

## 0:14 – Waits (8 s)
**On screen:** health-centre appointment: Finland **14 days**, SE · DK · NO 2–7 · GP → specialist: Finland **2–4 weeks**, SE · DK · NO 1–2 · specialist → diagnosis: Finland 4 weeks, Sweden also 4 · doctor at first contact: Finland **40–60%**, Sweden 80–100%, others 100%
**Say:** Finland waits longer: 14 days for a health-centre appointment, 2 to 4 weeks for a specialist. The delay comes **early**.

## 0:22 – Outcomes (7 s)
**On screen:** **No** national register · **0 of 8** hospital respondents report survival tracking · **35%** waited over 14 days for a doctor (2023)
**Say:** No national register. No hospital survival tracking. And 35 percent waited over two weeks for a **doctor**.

## 0:29 – What a county twin is (15 s)
**On screen:** map, one twin per county · Lapland · four steps: Detection, Referral, Diagnosis, Treatment · chart 2019–2020: dashed line = Finland + 10 points · about **0:40**, **GAP** lights on Referral
**Say:** So we built a twin for every county: a model of its cancer pathway, never a patient. Detection, referral, diagnosis, treatment: each watched monthly with public figures. When a step is clearly worse than Finland's, it flags a **gap**.

## 0:44 – Twin, live: Lapland (15 s)
**On screen:** clock 2016 → 2026 · four circles with % · "… waiting" under each stream · from about **0:53 to 0:56** the clock slows on Oct 2020 – Jan 2021: Referral **31.4%**, **GAP** · paper panel below: the same referral wait, peak 31%
**Say:** Here is Lapland, picked at random, replayed from 2016. Dots are patients waiting; dark dots, over 90 days. In late 2020, 31 percent wait over 90 days for a first assessment: the twin flags a **gap**.

## 0:59 – Who got help (17 s)
**On screen:** grey = county behind Finland · navy = Finland Cancer Plan Simulation · white = Simulation Using County Twins · gauges climb · about **1:11**, card: **20% → 51%**, +32 points (80% interval +19 to +45), stretches reached 3 → 13 of 19
**Say:** Both get the same three fast-track pilots. Grey: a county falling behind. Navy: the Finland Cancer Plan Simulation, once a year, on two-year-old figures. White: the Simulation Using County Twins, every month. A pilot was in place in **51** percent of those months, against 20.

## 1:16 – Wins and ties (10 s)
**On screen:** Referral **20% → 51% WIN** (+32) · Endoscopy 34% → 38% TIE (+4) · Oncology 56% → 60% TIE (+4) · misses: forecasts 10 of 10 and 5 of 10 · 2020 dip not flagged · oncology a tie
**Say:** A clear **win** on referral, plus 32 points. Endoscopy and oncology: ties, plus 4. And we show the misses: the 2020 dip was not flagged.

## 1:26 – Inputs (8 s)
**On screen:** THL queues, monthly from 2021 · Sotkanet screening, yearly · publication gate **+45 days** · footnote: THL = Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare
**Say:** All inputs are public, mostly THL, Finland's health institute. A figure enters only once published: **45 days** after a queue snapshot.

## 1:34 – Judge (8 s)
**On screen:** example North Ostrobothnia, endoscopy, June 2021: county **29.5%**, Finland 15%, threshold 25% · **P(gap) 0.90** · checks: interval 8 pts, data 2 months old, 175 waiting · → GAP
**Say:** Is it worse than Finland by a margin, and how sure? Here 29.5 against 25, 90 percent sure: **gap**.

## 1:42 – Status (7 s)
**On screen:** GAP · WATCH · OK · CANNOT JUDGE · example: Lapland, October 2020, **31% vs 13%** → GAP
**Say:** The answer is one word. Lapland, October 2020: 31 percent against 13 nationally. **Gap**.

## 1:49 – Conclusion (11 s)
**On screen:** 1 the paper · 2 the county twins · 3 **51%** vs 20% · not shown: any effect on survival
**Say:** The paper points at referral. County twins watch it everywhere, every month: same pilots, **51** percent against 20. No survival claim. Next: primary-care access.
