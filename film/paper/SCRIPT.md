# Voice-over script: paper_film.mp4 (2:00)

About 290 words, roughly 2.4 words per second. Each block starts when its slide appears. **Bold** marks the word to land on the reveal.

---

**0:00 – Title** (7 s)
Equal Care Across Finland. We start from one paper: a Nordic comparison of lung-cancer care.

**0:07 – Survival** (7 s)
Finland has the **lowest** lung-cancer survival in the Nordics, and finds the fewest cancers at stage one.

**0:14 – Waits** (8 s)
Finland waits longest at every step. The gap opens early: getting seen by a doctor, and getting **referred** to a specialist.

**0:22 – Outcomes** (7 s)
No national register. No hospital survival tracking. A third wait over two weeks for a doctor. Feedback is **slow**.

**0:29 – What a county twin is** (15 s)
So we built a twin for every county. Not a patient: a small model of the county's cancer pathway. Detection, referral, diagnosis, treatment, each watched with public figures. Every month the twin updates, and when a step is clearly worse than Finland's, it flags a **gap**.

**0:44 – Twin, live: Lapland** (15 s)
Here is Lapland, picked at random, replayed from 2016. The dots are patients waiting between steps; the dark ones have waited over ninety days. In late 2020 the referral wait jumps above Finland's, and the twin flags a **gap** as soon as the figure is published.

**0:59 – Who got help** (17 s)
Now give both approaches the same three fast-track pilots. Grey is a county falling behind. Navy is the Finland Cancer Plan Simulation, deciding once a year on two-year-old figures. White is the Simulation Using County Twins, deciding every month. Help was in place in **fifty-one** percent of the months that mattered, against twenty.

**1:16 – Wins and ties** (10 s)
The win is on referral, the step the paper flagged. Endoscopy and the oncology queue are ties. We also show where the model falls short.

**1:26 – Inputs** (8 s)
Everything comes from public, aggregate data, mainly THL, Finland's health institute, and is used only after it is published.

**1:34 – Judge** (8 s)
For each county, the twin asks: is it worse than Finland by more than a margin, and how sure are we?

**1:42 – Status** (7 s)
The answer is one word: gap, watch, OK, or cannot judge.

**1:49 – Conclusion** (11 s)
The paper points at referral. County twins watch that step everywhere, every month, and put the same pilots where they are needed. We do not claim an effect on survival. Next: primary-care access.
