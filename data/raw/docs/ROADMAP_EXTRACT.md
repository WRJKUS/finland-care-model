### Is screening outreach / reminders / invitations to non-participants funded?

**No.** None of measures 2.3.1–2.3.4 or 2.4.1–2.4.5, nor their resource (Resurssi) lines, mention reminders, re-invitations, or outreach to screening non-participants. The 80 % participation target is the indicator of 2.4.1 only, whose 350,000 € is for expert groups and course-format quality manuals. The words for reminder (muistutus) or re-invitation do not occur anywhere in the roadmap; 'kutsu-' appears in 2.3/2.4 only for (a) invitation intervals of the extended breast programme (p. 46), (b) the lung pilot inviting the EU target population (p. 47), (c) risk-based changes to who is invited and how often (pp. 49–50). 2.4.4 (2 500 000 € pilot R&D) is generic and does not name outreach. The closest funded item is outside 2.3/2.4: measure 1.3.1 (400 000 €, p. 33; cost table p. 104), targeted community-based communication to raise vaccination and screening participation of language groups. The equality chapter does acknowledge the problem (p. 11): "Maahan muuttaneiden osallistuminen syöpäseulontoihin on alhaisempaa kuin Suomessa syntyneiden. Osallistumattomuudelle on useita syitä."

EN: "Reliable and comprehensive data from recorded first symptoms to diagnosis are a precondition for detecting cancers earlier. These data are, however, not collected systematically.
