# Questions for the mentor (Kaisa Kaitila, Medical Affairs)

Each answer changes something specific in the model. Log answers in `SOURCES.md` as "mentor interview, role, date, time".

1. **Which delay do you see most in practice: before referral, referral to diagnosis, or diagnosis to first treatment, and for which cancers?** Changes which pathway step leads the demo.
2. **Are time-to-treatment figures recorded and published anywhere per county (hospital dashboards, FICAN reports, THL Ohjaus 21/2016 follow-up)?** Would replace our oncology-queue proxy with the real Goal 3 indicator.
3. **Does the THL "oncology (65) waiting for treatment" queue hold first treatments, or mostly follow-up and radiotherapy bookings?** Decides whether our Goal 3 proxy is valid at all.
4. **Our data shows chronic oncology queue pressure in HUS and North Karelia, and a first-assessment backlog in Päijät-Häme. Do these match what you hear? Which counties would you call outliers, and why?** External check of the gaps.
5. **For suspected cancer, do referrals bypass the general assessment queue (urgent track)?** If yes, `ref_assess_over90` overstates the cancer problem and should be demoted to context.
6. **Which roadmap measure do you expect to slip first: 2.5.2 referral criteria, 3.1.2 target times, or the 3.2.1 quality register?** Changes Model 3's assumed monitoring lag.
7. **How many county pilots of the pathway measures are realistic by 2028?** Replaces our assumed budget of three concurrent slots.
8. **Would a county act on a monthly signal, or is the decision cycle annual regardless?** If annual regardless, Model 4's advantage cannot be realised.
